## Short social post

> Block YouTube ads. Revive your old TV. JetsonCast turns a spare Ubuntu computer into a TV upgrade: YouTube ad blocking from VacuumTube, casting from your phone, and animated screensavers between videos. Even a TV without smart apps can join in. Built and tested on Jetson Orin Nano 8GB; no Jetson required.
