# Credits and distribution

Jetson Cast is an independent setup and customization layer for
[VacuumTube](https://github.com/shy1132/VacuumTube), by shy and contributors.
VacuumTube supplies the YouTube TV interface integration, DIAL discovery and
ad blocking. We do not claim those upstream features as our invention.

The installer retrieves the official pinned 2.1.1 release and verifies its
SHA256. No upstream runtime, account profile or browser data is redistributed
in this repository. The downloaded release retains its upstream LICENSE and
Electron/Chromium third-party notices. VacuumTube is MIT licensed:
Copyright (c) 2025–2026 shy. The MIT permission and warranty terms in LICENSE
also apply to any adapted upstream snippets, with this copyright retained.

The four ambient GIFs are AI-generated landscape artwork, assembled from
generated animation frames. They contain no photographs of real users or
device screenshots. They may be reused with this project under its MIT terms
to the extent copyright or other applicable rights exist. Their included
resolution is 1920×1080; the receiver scales them to the display.

of their respective owners. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed
